# Contribute a useful method

Contributions are welcome. Propose a concrete user outcome, the strongest simpler
alternative, exact inputs/results, collaborator contracts and required authority.
Keep one useful method independently consumable; do not add collection-wide
runtime policy or require adoption of Jig internals.

Each package needs readable source, ordinary dependencies, a license,
package-specific maintainers, focused tests, public usage and evidence with known
limits. Record failures as well as successes. Tests with controlled Agents do not
prove model judgment or host containment. Do not introduce credentials or private
records into fixtures or artifacts.

Run `just check` and exercise a complete packed package from outside this
repository. A new host, live model or comparative claim needs its own evidence.
Submitting a contribution confirms you may distribute it under the package's
stated license; retained third-party material keeps its attribution and terms.
